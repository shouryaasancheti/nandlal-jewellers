Drop your optional 3D jewellery GLB model here as:

  /public/models/hero.glb

When present, the Nandlal Jewellers hero and 360° viewer automatically load
the interactive 3D GLB model via Three.js GLTFLoader with drag-to-rotate support.

(SKU FA7BDH) with layered depth parallax and multi-angle real photo fallback.
